Small task. Use the quick flow:

1. Classify the request; delegate at most once to the most specific specialist.
2. Require the verification command and its raw output in the report; re-read the changed files yourself.
3. Report changed paths with `path:line`, the command output, and what you left out.
4. If the task needs more than one delegation, stop and say it belongs to `orchestrator`.
